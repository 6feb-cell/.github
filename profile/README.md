## 🔧 What Are HP Printer Drivers?

A **printer driver** is a piece of software that allows your operating system and applications to communicate effectively with your printer. Without the correct driver, your printer might not work properly, may be unable to print at all, or may not access advanced features like scanning or double-sided printing.

HP offers a wide range of printers including LaserJet, DeskJet, OfficeJet, ENVY, and Smart Tank models. Each model may require a specific driver version, especially if you're running a particular operating system like Windows 10/11, macOS, or Linux.
